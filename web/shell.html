How to survive

  • Find every key, then reach the glowing EXIT door.
  • Your flashlight drains. Spare batteries lie in the dark.
  • With the light on, they see you from far away.
  • They hear you run. Walking is quieter.
  • Listen to your heartbeat: it knows how close they are.
  • Three lives. Five levels. No way back.
